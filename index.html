How It Works

Learn → Train → Assess → Certify → Verify

Whether you join independently or attend a physical DIRI session, you use the same learner account and the same certification pathway.

01

Learn

Create or activate one DIRI account and study practical learning content online.

02

Train

Continue independently or take part in DIRI training at a workplace, school, university, NGO or community.

03

Assess

Complete the required quizzes or assessment. Physical participation and online results belong to the same record.

04

Certify

Qualifying learners are reviewed and approved before a DIRI Digital Safety Certificate is issued. Payment never guarantees approval.

05

Verify

Issued certificates support a unique number, QR code and public verification result. No certificate is shown unless it exists in DIRI's system.

Earn a verifiable DIRI certificate

Complete the required learning or training, pass the assessment and receive an approved DIRI Digital Safety Certificate.
