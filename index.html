Why DIRI exists

Digital life creates risks that practical learning can reduce

People use mobile money, social platforms, online services and AI every day, but clear digital-safety knowledge is not equally accessible. DIRI connects learning, training, assessment and support in one place.

Practical digital safety

Short learning cards and videos cover privacy, scams, secure accounts, data protection, cybersecurity and responsible online participation.

Assessment that matters

Lesson checks and weekly challenges help learners test understanding before qualification is considered.

Current awareness

DIRI updates connect learning to cyber trends, online-safety alerts, digital rights and internet-governance discussions.

Challenges and progress

Weekly quizzes, progress records and the leaderboard keep learning active without separating online and physically trained learners.

Guidance when needed

Mr. DIRI provides limited digital-safety assistance on the website, while DIRI on WhatsApp extends access through familiar messaging.

Inclusive access

Supported local languages, accessibility controls and a clearly labelled USSD prototype explore how more people can reach essential information.

How It Works

Learn → Train → Assess → Certify → Verify

Whether you join independently or attend a physical DIRI session, you use the same learner account and the same certification pathway.

01

Learn

Create or activate one DIRI account and study practical learning content online.

02

Train

Continue independently or take part in DIRI training at a workplace, school, university, NGO or community.

03

Assess

Complete the required quizzes or assessment. Physical participation and online results belong to the same record.

04

Certify

Qualifying learners are reviewed and approved before a DIRI Digital Safety Certificate is issued. Payment never guarantees approval.

05

Verify

Issued certificates support a unique number, QR code and public verification result. No certificate is shown unless it exists in DIRI's system.

Get DIRI certified

A digital certificate designed to be verifiable

After completing required learning or training and passing the applicable assessment, an approved learner can receive a DIRI Digital Safety Certificate.

  • Full learner name and certificate title
  • Unique certificate number and issue date
  • Certificate status
  • QR code and online verification URL
  • Download and print access from the learner dashboard
