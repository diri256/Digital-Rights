Privacy Policy

How DIRI collects, uses, and protects your personal data.

Last updated: July 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you create a DIRI account, we collect your email address and a display name. You may optionally provide a username and profile picture. We also collect data about your learning progress â€” which lessons you have completed, quiz scores, and leaderboard rankings.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your information to personalise your learning experience, track your progress, maintain the leaderboard, and improve our content. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a compliant cloud database platform. We implement industry-standard measures to protect your information from unauthorised access or disclosure.

4. Third-Party Services

DIRI uses Supabase for authentication and database services. Our WhatsApp chatbot is powered by a third-party messaging API. These services operate under their own privacy policies.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. Contact us to exercise these rights. You can also delete your account directly through your profile settings.

6. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please reach out to us via the contact page or through our social media channels.

DIRI is a youth-focused digital rights and internet governance platform built by Team Netizens, Mbarara University of Science and Technology.
