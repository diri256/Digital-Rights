IGF Hackathon

Where DIRI began.

The Internet Governance Forum (IGF) Hackathon is a global challenge that brings together innovators, developers, and policy enthusiasts to build solutions that address internet governance and digital rights challenges.

DIRI was created as Team Netizens' entry into the IGF Hackathon. The challenge was to design a platform that would make digital rights knowledge accessible and engaging for young people â€” especially those in communities where traditional policy discussions feel distant and inaccessible.

Over the course of the hackathon, the team designed and built DIRI from the ground up â€” combining interactive learning cards, a weekly quiz system, a leaderboard, and an AI-powered chatbot to create a complete digital rights learning experience.

The IGF Impact

The IGF is the United Nations' platform for multi-stakeholder dialogue on internet governance issues. By participating in the IGF Hackathon, Team Netizens joined a global community working to make the internet more inclusive, secure, and rights-respecting for all.

DIRI continues to evolve beyond the hackathon, with new lessons, features, and language support added regularly.

DIRI is a youth-focused digital rights and internet governance platform built by Team Netizens, Mbarara University of Science and Technology.
