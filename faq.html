Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about DIRI.

What is DIRI?

DIRI is a youth-focused digital rights and internet governance platform. It offers learning cards, weekly quiz challenges, a leaderboard, and an AI chatbot called Mr. DIRI â€” all designed to help young Ugandans understand their digital rights.

Is DIRI free?

Yes. DIRI is completely free to use. Create an account and access all lessons, quizzes, and features at no cost.

Who is DIRI for?

DIRI is built for young Ugandans â€” students, young professionals, and anyone interested in learning about digital rights, online privacy, data protection, cybersecurity, internet governance, and AI policy.

How do I get started?

Create a free account on the registration page. Once logged in, you can explore lessons, take quizzes, and track your progress on the leaderboard.

What is the weekly quiz challenge?

Every week, a new quiz goes live mixing lesson questions, current digital rights issues, and real-life scenarios. Your score and completion time are ranked on the leaderboard.

Can I use DIRI on WhatsApp?

Yes. You can chat with Mr. DIRI on WhatsApp by clicking the WhatsApp button on the homepage. Ask questions about digital rights and get answers in real time.

Are lessons available in local languages?

Yes. Key lessons and explanations are available in selected Ugandan local languages to make the content more accessible.

How is my data protected?

We take data protection seriously. Your information is stored securely, and we never sell your data. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

DIRI is a youth-focused digital rights and internet governance platform built by Team Netizens, Mbarara University of Science and Technology.
